BreedNote Privacy Policy

Local-first pet health log · No tracking, no account required

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Welcome to BreedNote. This privacy policy explains exactly what data we do (and do not) collect when you use the app. We built BreedNote as a local-first application: your pet's health records stay on your device by default, and we do not operate any user accounts.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

BreedNote does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or mailing address
  • Your pet's name, medical records, weight, or vaccine history
  • Photos you attach to clinic visits
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / IDFV on iOS, AAID on Android)
  • Analytics or crash telemetry from a first-party service
All pet data — breeds, vaccines, weight charts, visit notes, photos — is stored locally on your device using SQLite. It never leaves your phone or tablet unless you explicitly export it (for example, by sharing a PDF health report).

2. Permissions We Request

BreedNote requests the following device permissions, and only when you actually use the related feature:

PermissionPurposeData Leaves Device?
NotificationsDeliver vaccine, deworming, and wellness reminders you scheduleNo — local notifications only
Photo Library / CameraAttach photos to clinic visit notes (you choose which images)No — stored locally
Photo Library (Pro)Save exported PDF health reports to your deviceNo — saved locally

You can revoke any permission at any time through your device's Settings app. The core features of BreedNote continue to work without notifications or photo access.

4. Children's Privacy

BreedNote is rated for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). Since we do not request accounts, names, or any identifiers, there is nothing for us to knowingly collect.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not store your pet's data on our servers, there is nothing we can delete on your behalf. To remove all data from your device, simply uninstall BreedNote. Alternatively, you can use the in-app "Delete Pet" action on each pet profile.

6. International Use

BreedNote is currently available in the United States and Japan. Since no pet data is transmitted from your device, international transfer concerns do not apply to your health records. Subscription data handled by RevenueCat and the App Store / Google Play is subject to their respective policies.

7. Future Changes

We may add features in the future — such as optional cloud sync — that would require handling more data. If we do, we will update this policy, offer an explicit opt-in, and give you a clear way to disable cloud features at any time. We will never begin collecting data we do not need for a clearly stated purpose.
